背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第七条 民事主体从事民事活动，应当遵循诚信原则，秉持诚实，恪守承诺。

Article 7 When conducting a civil activity, a person of the civil law shall, in compliance with the principle of good faith, uphold honesty and honor commitments.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第七条，阐明了民事主体各方在从事民事活动时的主观诚实与客观守信。

This article, as Article 7 of the Civil Code, sets forth the internal honesty and external commitment-keeping of the persons of the civil law when engaging in civil activities.

诚信原则的两层含义具体如下：

The principle of good faith has two layers of meaning:

- 主观意思：当事人应怀有诚实、公正的意图，不欺骗、不隐瞒。

- Subjective intention: the parties shall harbor honest and fair intentions, and shall not deceive or conceal.

- 客观行为：在行使权利和履行义务时，应遵循公认的商业道德和公平标准。

- Objective conduct: in exercising rights and performing obligations, they shall follow generally accepted commercial morality and fairness standards.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.the principle of good faith 诚信原则

good faith是法律英语中的固定术语，意为“诚实信用”或“诚信”。

"Good faith" is a fixed term in legal English, meaning "honesty and credit" or "integrity and trustworthiness."

不可简译为honesty principle或integrity principle，前者过于口语，后者侧重廉洁而非法律上的善意。

It should not be simply translated as "honesty principle" or "integrity principle" — the former being too colloquial, and the latter emphasizing moral uprightness rather than legal good faith.

2.uphold honesty 秉持诚实

uphold意为“坚持、维护、秉持”，比keep或maintain更有法律和道德上的庄重感。

"Uphold" means "to maintain, to adhere to, or to stand by," and carries a greater sense of legal and moral gravity than "keep" or "maintain."

honesty对应“诚实”，指主观上不欺诈、不隐瞒真实情况。

"Honesty" corresponds to "honesty" or "being honest," referring to the subjective refraining from fraud or concealment of the true situation.

此处强调诚信原则的“主观方面”。

Here, it emphasizes the "subjective aspect" of the principle of good faith.

3.honor commitments 恪守承诺

honor作动词时意为“遵守、履行、兑现”，常用于履行合同、偿还债务。

"Honor" as a verb means "to abide by, to perform, or to fulfill," and is often used in the context of performing a contract or repaying a debt.

commitments指“承诺、约定”，涵盖合同义务、单方允诺及其他法律上应遵守的约定。

"Commitments" refer to "promises or undertakings," covering contractual obligations, unilateral promises, and other legally binding engagements.

此处强调诚信原则的“客观方面”。

Here, it emphasizes the "objective aspect" of the principle of good faith.
