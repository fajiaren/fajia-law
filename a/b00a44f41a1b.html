背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十条 处理民事纠纷，应当依照法律；法律没有规定的，可以适用习惯，但是不得违背公序良俗。

Article 10 Civil disputes shall be resolved in accordance with law. Where the law does not specify, custom may be applied, provided that public order and good morals may not be offended.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条确立了我国民法的法律渊源规则，规定了处理民事纠纷时的法律适用顺序：

This article establishes the rules on sources of law for China's civil law and sets forth the order of legal application in handling civil disputes:

1. 法律优先：处理民事纠纷，首先应当依照法律（制定法）的规定

I. Primacy of law:civil disputes shall first be resolved in accordance with law (statutory law).

2. 习惯补充：在法律没有规定的情况下，可以适用习惯（作为补充法源）

II. Supplement by custom:in the absence of statutory provisions, custom may be applied as a supplementary source of law.

3. 公序良俗限制：适用习惯时，不得违背公序良俗——这是对习惯适用的外部限制

III. Limitation by public order and good morals:in applying custom, it may not offend public order or good morals — this constitutes an external limitation on the application of custom.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.civil disputes 民事纠纷

civil disputes指民事主体之间因人身关系或财产关系发生的争议。

"Civil disputes" refer to disputes arising between the persons of the civil law over personal relationships or property relationships.

本条规定的是“处理”民事纠纷的方式，适用于诉讼、仲裁、调解等多种争议解决途径。

This article prescribes the method of "resolving" civil disputes, which is applicable to a variety of dispute resolution mechanisms, including litigation, arbitration, and mediation.

2.in accordance with law 依照法律

in accordance with law是法律英语中表示“依照法律”的固定用语，已在第一条中详细解读。

"In accordance with law" is a fixed expression in legal English meaning "in compliance with the law," and has been discussed in detail in the interpretation of Article 1.

此处law指制定法，即国家立法机关制定的法律（包括法律、行政法规等），不包括习惯或道德规范。

Here, "law" refers to statutory law, i.e., laws enacted by the national legislature (including laws, administrative regulations, etc.), and does not include custom or moral norms.

3.Where the law does not specify 若没有法律规定

specify意为“明确规定、具体说明”，强调法律对某一事项有明确的条文规定。

"Specify" means "to clearly provide or to state expressly," emphasizing that the law has a clear provision on a particular matter.

与 does not provide相比，does not specify更侧重于“缺乏具体规定”，而不仅仅是“未作规定”。

Compared with "does not provide," "does not specify" places greater emphasis on the absence of a specific provision, rather than merely the absence of any provision at all.

4.custom n.习惯

custom指在一定范围内经长期实践形成、为公众所认可并具有约束力的行为规则。

"Custom" refers to a rule of conduct that has been formed through long-standing practice within a certain sphere, recognized by the public, and has binding force.

custom（习惯法）：具有法律约束力的习惯，如商事惯例、地方习俗。

"Custom" (habitual law) refers to binding practices, such as commercial practices and local customs.

usage（惯例）：通常指行业或交易中的惯常做法，不一定具有法律约束力。

"Usage" generally refers to customary practices in a trade or transaction, which are not necessarily binding.

5.may be applied 可以适用

may表示授权性规范，即 “可以”但非“必须”。即使法律没有规定，法院也有权裁量是否适用习惯，而非必须适用。

"May" denotes permissive norm，meaning "is allowed but not required." Even where the law does not provide, the court has the discretion to determine whether to apply custom, rather than being obligated to do so.

applied意为“适用”，指将习惯作为裁判依据援引。

"Applied" means "to be used as the basis for adjudication," referring to the invocation of custom as the ground for decision-making.

6.provided that 但是……除外，以……为条件

provided that在法律英语中常用于引入条件、例外或限制性条款。

"Provided that" is frequently used in legal English to introduce conditions, exceptions, or restrictive clauses.

与 however或 but相比，provided that更正式，更强调“附条件”。

Compared with "however" or "but," "provided that" is more formal and places greater emphasis on conditionality.

7.may not be offended 不得违背

may not be offended：虽然形式上用了 may，但与否定词 not结合，构成禁止性规范，意为“不得触犯/违背”。

"May not be offended": Although it takes the form of "may," when combined with the negative "not," it constitutes a prohibitive norm, meaning "shall not be contravened or violated."

此处使用被动式 may not be offended，强调习惯本身不能违反公序良俗，而非强调行为主体。

Here, the passive construction "may not be offended" is used to emphasize that custom itself must not contravene public order or good morals, rather than emphasizing the actor.
