背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第一条 为了保护民事主体的合法权益，调整民事关系，维护社会和经济秩序，适应中国特色社会主义发展要求，弘扬社会主义核心价值观，根据宪法，制定本法。

Article 1 This Law is formulated in accordance with the Constitution of the People’s Republic of China for the purposes of protecting the lawful rights and interests of the persons of the civil law, regulating civil-law relations, maintaining social and economic order, meeting the needs for developing socialism with Chinese characteristics, and carrying forward the core socialist values.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为本法的首条，说明了本法的规范对象、立法目的与立法依据。

This article, as the first article of this Law, sets forth the objects regulated, the legislative purposes, and the legislative basis of this Law.

本法的规范对象：民事主体间的民事关系。

The objects regulated by this Law：civil-law relations among the persons of the civil law.

本法的立法目的为：

（1）直接目的：保护民事主体的合法权益。

（2）宏观目的：维护社会和经济秩序，适应中国特色社会主义发展要求，弘扬社会主义核心价值观。

The legislative purposes of this Law are:

(1) Immediate purpose: protecting the lawful rights and interests of the persons of the civil law;

(2) Macro-level purpose: maintaining social and economic order, meeting the needs for developing socialism with Chinese characteristics, and carrying forward the core socialist values.

本法的立法依据：中华人民共和国宪法。

The legislative basis of this Law: Constitution of the People's Republic of China.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.this Law 本法

在法律文本中，通常以首字母大写的格式指代文本本身。故在此处指代“民法典”。

In legal texts, it is customary to refer to the text itself by using initial capitals. Hence, in the present context, it refers to the Civil Code.

2.formulate 制定（法律）

指经过研究、论证，将法律原则、规则以条理化、体系化的文字形式拟定出来。

It refers to the process of formulating legal principles and rules in a coherent and systematic written form through research and deliberation.

此处选用 formulate，侧重说明立法依据（合宪性）和法典的整体构建，而非强调“表决通过”“予以颁布”或“公布施行”。

The term herein "formulate" is chosen to emphasize the legislative basis (constitutionality) and the overall structure of the Code, rather than focusing on "adopt," "enact," or "promulgate."

3.in accordance with 根据（法律）

指表示某种行为、文件或规范符合、遵从某个上位法、规则或标准。

It denotes that a certain act, document, or norm conforms to and complies with a superior law, rule, or standard.

“依据……制定（法律/合同）”固定用formulated in accordance with。

The fixed expression for “formulated in accordance with” when referring to the enactment of a law or contract is “formulated in accordance with.”

若强调某种行为系遵照合同、法规的某一条款而授权执行时，选用pursuant to更佳。

"Pursuant to" is better, if the emphasis is on an act being carried out by a spcific clauase of an article of law or contract.

4.Constitution of the People’s Republic of China （中华人民共和国）宪法

中国的官方称呼为“中华人民共和国”，此处使用了中国宪法的正式全称。

"People’s Republic of China" is the official designation of China. "Constitution of the People's Republic of China" is the full formal title of "Constitution".

5.for the purpose of 为了（目的）

专用于引出立法目的、制定意图或适用目标。

It is used exclusively to introduce legislative purposes, legislative intent, or applicable objectives.

在某一条款的内部，若需限定适用对象，常用for the purpose(s) of this section。

Within a particular provision, when it is necessary to limit the scope of application, the phrase "for the purpose(s) of this section" is commonly used.

6.lawful rights and interests 合法权益

指法律所承认并保护的权利和利益，涵盖既有的法定权利和受法律认可的利益。

It refers to the rights and interests that are recognized and protected by law, encompassing both existing statutory rights and interests that are legally acknowledged.

lawful更侧重“法律上可允许的、不违法的”，强调实质合法性；而legal更侧重“法律形式上的、符合法律规定的”。

"Lawful" emphasizes "permissible under law and not unlawful," highlighting substantive legality, while "legal" focuses more on "formal conformity with statutory provisions.

7.persons of the civil law 民事主体

指依法能够享有民事权利和承担民事义务的法律人格者，即参与民事法律关系的主体。

It refers to those legal persons who, in accordance with law, are capable of enjoying civil rights and assuming civil obligations, i.e., the subjects participating in civil legal relations.

“persons”体现“主体”的法律人格属性，涵盖自然人、法人和非法人组织，比“ subjects”更为准确。

"Persons" reflects the legal personality of the subjects and covers natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations, making it more accurate than "subjects."

8.regulate 调整（立法规范对象）

指通过规则系统地进行调整、规范某种社会关系或具体行为，如某一法律对哪些领域或关系施加影响。

It refers to the systematic adjustment and regulation of certain social relations or specific conduct through rules, such as the influence that a law exerts over particular areas or relationships.

govern常用于上位规则对下位规则的影响，如 the contract is governed by Chinese law（合同受中国法律管辖）。

The term "govern" is often used to denote the influence of superior rules over inferior rules, for example, "the contract is governed by Chinese law" (i.e., the contract is subject to Chinese law).

9.civil-law relations 民事关系

指平等主体（民事主体）之间的人身关系和财产关系，是民法调整的基本对象。

It refers to the personal and property relations among equal subjects（persons of the civil law）, and constitutes the fundamental object of regulation under civil law.

使用带连字符的 civil-law可以明确限定为“民法上的”，与“civil legal relations”可能引发的歧义理解（公民的、民事的）相区分。

The hyphenated term "civil-law" clearly delimits it as "pertaining to civil law," thereby distinguishing it from "civil legal relations," which may give rise to ambiguity and be misconstrued as "citizen's" or "civil" in a general sense.

10.socialism with Chinese characteristics 中国特色社会主义

指中国共产党和中国人民在长期实践中形成的、符合中国国情和社会发展阶段的社会主义发展道路和理论体系。

It refers to the path of socialist development and the theoretical system formed by the Communist Party of China and the Chinese people through long-term practice, which are consistent with China's national conditions and its stage of development.

11.core socialist values 社会主义核心价值观

指在中国共产党领导下，全社会共同认同并倡导的价值理念体系。

It refers to the system of values and principles that are commonly recognized and advocated by the whole society under the leadership of the Communist Party of China.
