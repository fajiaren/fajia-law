背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第九条 民事主体从事民事活动，应当有利于节约资源、保护生态环境。

Article 9 When conducting a civil activity, a person of the civil law shall act in a manner that facilitates conservation of resources and protection of the ecological environment.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第九条，阐明了民事主体各方在从事民事活动时的绿色原则。

This article, as Article 9 of the Civil Code, sets forth the green principle for the persons of the civil law when engaging in civil activities.

绿色原则（即生态保护原则）：民事主体在从事民事活动时，应当以有利于节约资源和保护环境的方式行事，将环境保护义务融入民事活动的全过程不得以牺牲生态环境为代价追求经济利益。

The green principle (also known as the ecological protection principle) requires that civil subjects, when conducting civil activities, shall act in a manner that facilitates conservation of resources and protection of the ecological environment, integrate environmental protection obligations throughout the entire process of civil activities, and shall not pursue economic interests at the expense of the ecological environment.

绿色原则属于倡导性规范，而非禁止性或强制性规范。它不直接规定“不得浪费资源”或“不得污染环境”，而是要求民事主体在行为方式上选择有利于资源节约和环境保护的方案。

The green principle is an encouraging norm, rather than a prohibitive or mandatory one. It does not directly prescribe that “resources shall not be wasted” or “the environment shall not be polluted,” but rather requires civil subjects to choose, in their mode of conduct, solutions that favor resource conservation and environmental protection.

在具体法律适用中，若合同约定或行为方式明显违反绿色原则，法院可据此进行裁量（如认定合同履行方式不合理，或对损害生态环境的行为施加侵权责任）。

In specific legal application, if a contractual stipulation or mode of conduct clearly violates the green principle, the court may exercise its discretion accordingly (for example, by finding the manner of contract performance unreasonable, or by imposing tort liability for acts that harm the ecological environment).

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.act in a manner that facilitates 以……方式行事，有利于

facilitates意为“促进、有助于、使便利”，比 promote（推动）更中性，比 contribute to（贡献）更具体，强调行为对某一目标的正向推动作用。

"Facilitates" means "to promote, to contribute to, or to make easier." It is more neutral than "promote" and more specific than "contribute to," emphasizing the positive effect of an act toward a certain goal.

整个短语准确传达了“以有利于……的方式行事”的规范要求，属于倡导性规范。

The full phrase accurately conveys the normative requirement of "acting in a manner that is conducive to..." and falls under the category of encouraging norms.

2.conservation of resources 节约资源

conservation指“节约、保护、保存”，强调对资源的合理利用和避免浪费。在法律英语中，conservation常与自然资源、能源等搭配。

"Conservation" refers to "saving, protecting, and preserving," with emphasis on the rational use of resources and the avoidance of waste. In legal English, "conservation" is often used in conjunction with "natural resources," "energy," and the like.

resources指“资源”，包括自然资源（如矿产、森林、水）、能源资源、物质资源等。

"Resources" refers to "resources," including natural resources (such as minerals, forests, and water), energy resources, and material resources.

3.protection of the ecological environment 保护生态环境

ecological environment是官方译法，比单独使用 environment更具体，强调生态系统的整体性和关联性。

"Ecological environment" is the official translation. It is more specific than the use of "environment" alone, emphasizing the integrity and interconnectedness of the ecosystem.

选用 ecological environment与我国生态文明建设的官方表述（ecological civilization）保持一致，体现国家政策导向。

The adoption of "ecological environment" is consistent with the official expression of China's ecological civilization development, namely "ecological civilization," and reflects the orientation of national policy.
