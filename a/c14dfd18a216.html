背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十七条 十八周岁以上的自然人为成年人。不满十八周岁的自然人为未成年人。

Article 17 A natural person aged eighteen or above is an adult. A natural person under the age of eighteen is a minor.

01【条款解读】Provision Interpretation

本条确立了本条确立了自然人的成年与未成年划分标准，以十八周岁为界。

This article establishes the criteria for distinguishing adults from minors among natural persons, with the age of eighteen as the dividing line.

核心含义Core Meaning

成年人：十八周岁以上的自然人。

Adult: a natural person aged eighteen or above.

未成年人：不满十八周岁的自然人。

Minor: a natural person under the age of eighteen.

本条是《民法典》总则编中关于自然人年龄身份的基础性规定，为后续民事行为能力制度、监护制度、婚姻家庭制度等提供了年龄划分依据。

This article is a foundational provision in the General Provisions of the Civil Code concerning the age status of natural persons, and provides the age-based classification basis for the subsequent system of capacity for civil conduct, the guardianship system, the marriage and family system, and others.

02【术语解释】Terminology Explanation

1. adult n. 成年人

adult 在法律英语中指达到法定成年年龄的自然人。

In legal English, "adult" refers to a natural person who has reached the statutory age of majority.

年满十八周岁的成年人原则上具有完全民事行为能力，但若因精神状况等原因，仍可能被认定为限制或无民事行为能力人。

A natural person aged eighteen or above is, in principle, a person with full capacity for civil conduct; however, due to mental condition or other reasons, such a person may still be determined to be a person with limited or no capacity for civil conduct.

2. aged eighteen or above 十八周岁以上

3. under the age of eighteen 不满十八周岁

4. minor n. 未成年人

minor 是法律英语中“未成年人”的标准术语。

"Minor" is the standard term in legal English for a "未成年人."

minor 原则上为限制民事行为能力人（八周岁以上）或无民事行为能力人（不满八周岁）,由其法定代理人代理或同意、追认实施民事法律行为。

In principle, a minor is either a person with limited capacity for civil conduct (aged eight or above) or a person with no capacity for civil conduct (under the age of eight), and shall perform civil juristic acts through a statutory agent or with the consent or ratification of such agent.
