背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十五条 自然人的出生时间和死亡时间，以出生证明、死亡证明记载的时间为准；没有出生证明、死亡证明的，以户籍登记或者其他有效身份登记记载的时间为准。有其他证据足以推翻以上记载时间的，以该证据证明的时间为准。

Article 15 The time of birth and time of death of a natural person are determined by the time recorded on his birth or death certificate as applied, or, if there is no birth or death certificate, by the time recorded in the natural person’s household registration or other valid identity certificate. If there is sufficient evidence overturning the time recorded in the aforementioned documents, the time that is established by such evidence shall prevail.

01【条款解读】Provision Interpretation

本条确立了自然人出生时间和死亡时间的确定规则，是民事权利能力起止时间（第十三条）的证据法配套条款。

This article establishes the rules for determining the time of birth and time of death of a natural person, and serves as the evidentiary supporting provision to Article 13 (which prescribes the commencement and termination of capacity for enjoying civil-law rights).

核心含义Core Meaning

第一顺位证据：以出生证明或死亡证明记载的时间为准（证明优先原则）。

Primary Evidence:the time recorded on the birth or death certificate shall prevail (the principle of proof priority).

第二顺位证据：在无上述证明时，以户籍登记或其他有效身份登记记载的时间为准。

Secondary Evidence:in the absence of such certificates, the time recorded in the household registration or other valid identity certificate shall prevail.

反证优先规则：有其他证据足以推翻上述记载时间的，以该证据证明的时间为准（实质真实原则）。

Rebuttal Rule:where there is other sufficient evidence overturning the time recorded in the aforementioned documents, the time established by such evidence shall prevail (the principle of substantive truth).

本条体现了法律对证据效力层级的安排，兼顾了登记的权威性与实质真实的追求。

This article reflects the legislative arrangement of hierarchical evidentiary weight, striking a balance between the authority of registration and the pursuit of substantive truth.

02【术语解释】Terminology Explanation

1. birth or death certificate 出生证明/死亡证明

birth certificate是由医疗机构或有关部门出具的、记载新生儿出生信息的法定证明文件。

A birth certificate is a legal evidentiary document issued by a medical institution or relevant authority, recording the birth information of a newborn.

death certificate是由医疗机构或有关部门出具的、记载自然人死亡信息的法定证明文件。

A death certificate is a legal evidentiary document issued by a medical institution or relevant authority, recording the death information of a natural person.

2. household registration 户籍登记

household registration指中国特有的户籍管理制度（俗称“户口”），由公安机关负责登记和管理。

"Household registration" refers to the household registration system unique to China (commonly known as "hukou"), which is administered and managed by the public security authorities.

3. evidence n. 证据

evidence指用以证明案件事实真伪的各类材料或信息，包括书证、物证、证人证言、鉴定意见、勘验笔录、视听资料、电子数据等。

"Evidence" refers to all types of materials or information used to prove the truth or falsity of facts in a case, including documentary evidence, physical evidence, witness testimony, expert opinions, inspection records, audio-visual materials, electronic data, and the like.

sufficient evidence设定了证明标准，仅有“证据”不足以推翻登记，证据必须达到“充分”的程度。

"Sufficient evidence" sets the standard of proof. Mere "evidence" is insufficient to overturn a registration; the evidence must reach the level of "sufficiency."

proof指证明/证明力，强调证据的证明效果或证明过程，如举证责任。

"Proof" refers to the effect or process of proving, emphasizing the evidentiary result or the procedural aspect of proof, as in "burden of proof."

testimony指证言，即证人当庭或书面所作的陈述。

"Testimony" refers to the statement made by a witness, either in court or in writing.

4. overturn v. 推翻

overturn在法律英语中意为“推翻、否定、撤销”，指以新的证据或理由否定既有事实认定或法律裁决的效力。"Overturn" in legal English means "to reverse, to nullify, or to set aside," referring to the invalidation of an existing factual finding or judicial decision by new evidence or new grounds.

在中国民事诉讼中，“足以推翻”通常要求证据达到高度盖然性标准，即证据证明的事实具有高度可能性。

In Chinese civil litigation, "sufficient to overturn" typically requires evidence to meet the standard of "high probability", i.e., the facts established by the evidence are highly probable.

rebut指反驳，即提出反证或相反论点，但不一定完全推翻，常用于可反驳的推定。"Rebut" means to contradict, i.e., to present counter-evidence or opposing arguments, but not necessarily to completely overturn. It is often used in the context of rebuttable presumptions.

overrule指推翻/驳回，特指上级法院推翻下级法院的判例或裁定。

"Overrule" means to overturn or to reject, and is specifically used to refer to a higher court overturning a precedent or ruling of a lower court.
