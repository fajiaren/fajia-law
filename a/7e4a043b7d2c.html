背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十四条 自然人的民事权利能力一律平等。

Article 14 All natural persons are equal in their capacity for enjoying civil-law rights.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条确立了自然人民事权利能力的平等原则。其核心含义为：

This article establishes the principle of equal capacity for enjoying civil-law rights for all natural persons. Its core meaning is as follows:

权利能力平等：所有自然人，不论其性别、年龄、民族、职业、财产状况、教育程度等，在民事权利能力上一律平等。

Equality of capacity for rights: all natural persons, regardless of gender, age, ethnicity, occupation, property status, educational level, or the like, are equal in their capacity for enjoying civil-law rights.

平等的内容：每个自然人都具有享有民事权利、承担民事义务的资格，这种资格不因个体差异而有高低优劣之分。

Content of equality: every natural person possesses the qualification to enjoy civil-law rights and assume civil-law obligations, and such qualification does not vary in degree or quality due to individual differences.

法律后果：任何组织和个人不得剥夺或限制自然人的民事权利能力（法律另有规定的除外）。

Legal consequence: no organization or individual may deprive or restrict the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights of a natural person, except as otherwise provided by law.

本条是宪法“法律面前人人平等”原则在民法领域的具体体现，是民事主体制度的核心价值之一。

This article embodies, in the field of civil law, the constitutional principle of “equality before the law,” and constitutes one of the core values of the system of civil subjects.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1. are equal in 在……方面平等

equal 在法律英语中意为“平等的、同等的”，强调无差别对待。

In legal English, "equal" means "fair and the same," emphasizing non-discriminatory treatment.
