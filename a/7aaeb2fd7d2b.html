背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十三条 自然人从出生时起到死亡时止，具有民事权利能力，依法享有民事权利，承担民事义务。

Article 13 A natural person shall, from the time of birth until the time of death, have the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights, and may enjoy civil-law rights and assume civil-law duties in accordance with law.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条确立了自然人的民事权利能力制度，规定了自然人享有民事权利、承担民事义务的资格的存续期间。

This article establishes the system of capacity for enjoying civil-law rights for natural persons, and prescribes the duration during which a natural person has the qualification to enjoy civil-law rights and assume civil-law duties.

权利能力的起止时间：始于出生，终于死亡。

Duration of capacity for rights:from the time of birth until the time of death.

权利能力的性质：是享有权利、承担义务的法律资格。

Nature of capacity for rights:it is the legal qualification to enjoy rights and assume obligations.

法律后果：自然人在其生命的全过程中（从出生到死亡），依法享有民事权利并承担民事义务。

Legal consequence:throughout the entire course of their life (from birth until death), natural persons enjoy civil-law rights and assume civil-law duties in accordance with law.

本条是《民法典》总则编中关于自然人的基础性规定，是民法主体制度的起点。

This article is a fundamental provision concerning natural persons in the General Provisions of the Civil Code, and serves as the point of departure for the system of civil law subjects.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1. birth n.出生

指自然人脱离母体并具有生命的时刻。关于“出生”的法律认定，通常以“独立呼吸说”为标准（即胎儿脱离母体后能够独立呼吸）。

It refers to the moment when a natural person leaves the mother's body and has life. In legal practice, the determination of "birth" usually follows the "independent respiration" standard, i.e., the fetus is able to breathe independently after being separated from the mother's body.

2.death n.死亡

指自然人生命终止的时刻。包括自然死亡（生理死亡）和宣告死亡（法律上推定死亡，但宣告死亡并不实际消灭权利能力，仅在法律上产生与自然死亡类似的效果）。

It refers to the moment when a natural person's life ends. This includes natural death (physiological death) and declaration of death (legal presumption of death, which does not actually extinguish the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights, but only produces legal effects similar to those of natural death).

3.capacity for enjoying civil-law rights 民事权利能力

capacity意为“能力、资格”，此处强调的是一种法律上的资格。

"Capacity" means "ability" or "qualification." In this context, it emphasizes a legal qualification.

enjoy意为“享有”，指对权利的拥有和行使。

"Enjoy" means "to have and exercise," referring to the possession and exercise of rights.

capacity for enjoying明确表示这是一种“享有……的资格”，即被动性的权利能力。

"Capacity for enjoying" clearly indicates that this is a qualification "to enjoy" something, i.e., a passive capacity for rights.

4.assume civil-law duties 承担民事义务

assume意为“承担”，指对义务的负担和履行。

"Assume" means "to undertake" or "to bear," referring to the burden and performance of obligations.

在法律英语中，assume与 obligations/duties 搭配是固定用法，强调“主动接受或负担”义务。

In legal English, "assume" is a fixed collocation with "obligations" or "duties," emphasizing the voluntary acceptance or bearing of obligations.

5.in accordance with law 依法

in accordance with law用于修饰主体的行为方式，表示该行为必须符合法律规定。

“In accordance with law” is used to modify the manner of conduct of a subject, indicating that such conduct must conform to legal requirements.

by law或 as provided by law强调规范由法律制定，说明“某事项由法律作出规定”。

“By law” or “as provided by law” emphasizes that the norm is established by law, and used to indicate that a matter is prescribed by law.

当表述“依据某具体的法律条款”时则用 pursuant to law。

When referring to “pursuant to a specific provision of law,” the term “pursuant to law” is used.
