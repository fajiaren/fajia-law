背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第四条 民事主体在民事活动中的法律地位一律平等。

Article 4 All persons of the civil law are equal in legal status when conducting civil activities.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第四条，阐明了民事主体在从事民事活动时的地位平等性。

This article, as Article 4 of the Civil Code, sets forth the equality of status among civil subjects when engaging in civil activities.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.conduct v. 从事，实施（法律行为）

Conduct侧重持续性、过程性、一整套活动，而engage较为口语化，perform则用于单次、特定的行为。

Conduct emphasizes continuous, procedural and a whole set of activities; engage is relatively colloquial, while perform applies to single and specific acts.

Conduct为中性术语，不分合法/非法，完美匹配我国“民事活动”概念，既包含合法的民事法律行为，也包含违法的事实行为。

Conduct is a neutral term irrespective of lawfulness or unlawfulness, which perfectly matches the concept of civil activities under Chinese law and covers both lawful civil juristic acts and unlawful factual acts.

2.civil activities 民事活动

民事活动是民事法律行为（civil juristic act）的上位概念，指所有民事主体参与私法层面交往的全部活动，包含买卖、租赁、占有物品、侵权、无因管理；范围宽泛，既有意思行为，也有事实行为。

Civil activities are the superordinate concept of civil juristic acts. It refers to all interactions conducted by civil subjects under private law, including sales, leases, possession of objects, torts and negotiorum gestio. It covers a broad scope, encompassing both intentional acts and factual acts.
