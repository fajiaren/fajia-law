背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第十六条 涉及遗产继承、接受赠与等胎儿利益保护的，胎儿视为具有民事权利能力。但是，胎儿娩出时为死体的，其民事权利能力自始不存在。

Article 16 A fetus is deemed as having the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights in estate succession, acceptance of gift, and other situations where protection of a fetus ’ interests is involved. Provided, however, that a stillborn fetus does not have such capacity ab initio.

01【条款解读】Provision Interpretation

本条确立了胎儿利益保护的特殊规则，是民事权利能力“始于出生”原则的例外规定。

This article establishes a special rule for the protection of fetal interests, and serves as an exception to the principle that the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights "begins at birth".

核心含义Core Meaning

原则：自然人的民事权利能力始于出生（《民法典》第十三条）。

Principle: the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights of a natural person begins at birth (Article 13 of the Civil Code).

例外：在涉及遗产继承、接受赠与等胎儿利益保护的情形下，胎儿视为具有民事权利能力。

Exception: in estate succession, acceptance of gift, and other situations where protection of a fetus' interests is involved, the fetus is deemed as having the capacity for enjoying civil-law rights.

限制：如果胎儿娩出时为死体，则其民事权利能力自始不存在（即法律上视为从未有过权利能力）。

Limitation: if the fetus is stillborn, such capacity does not exist ab initio (i.e., it is legally deemed as never having existed).

本条体现了民法对潜在生命和人格利益的保护，是民法人文关怀精神的集中体现。

This article embodies the protection of potential life and personality interests under civil law, and represents the concentrated expression of the humanistic concern inherent in civil law.

02【术语解释】Terminology Explanation

1. fetus n. 胎儿

fetus 指母体内的未出生生命体，即从受孕至出生前的生命形态。

"Fetus" refers to the unborn life form within the mother's body, i.e., the life form from conception to birth.

fetus 是医学术语，在法律中更为中性、准确，强调其“尚未出生”的生物学状态。

"Fetus" is a medical term, which is more neutral and precise in legal contexts, emphasizing its biological status of "not yet being born."

stillborn fetus 强调“娩出时为死体”的事实，故该权利能力即溯及既往地消灭。

"Stillborn fetus" emphasizes the fact that the fetus is dead at delivery so the capacity for rights is retroactively extinguished.

2. deemed as 视为

deemed as 是法律英语中的核心术语，意为“视为、被认定为”，用于法律拟制（legal fiction）。

"Deemed as" is a core term in legal English, meaning "regarded as" or "treated as," and is used in the context of legal fiction.

与 regarded as 或 considered as 相比，deemed as 在法律文本中更正式。

Compared with "regarded as" or "considered as," "deemed as" is more formal in legal texts.

法律拟制：将某一事实当作另一事实处理，即使两者在事实上不完全相同。

Legal fiction is to treat one fact as if it were another, even though the two are not completely identical in reality.

3. estate succession 遗产继承

estate 指“遗产”，即自然人死亡时遗留的个人合法财产。

"Estate" refers to the property, i.e., the lawful personal property left by a natural person at the time of death.

succession 指“继承”，即依法或依遗嘱将死者财产转移给继承人的制度。

"Succession" refers to inheritance, i.e., the legal system by which the property of the deceased is transferred to heirs in accordance with law or by testamentary disposition.

estate succession 涵盖法定继承和遗嘱继承。

"Estate succession" covers both statutory succession and testamentary succession.

4. acceptance of gift 接受赠与

gift 指“赠与”，即赠与人无偿将自己的财产给予受赠人的法律行为。

"Gift" refers to "donation," i.e., the juridical act by which the donor gratuitously transfers his property to the donee.

acceptance 指“接受”，是受赠人表示同意接受赠与的意思表示。

"Acceptance" refers to "acceptance," which is the declaration of will by the donee indicating consent to accept the gift.

赠与合同在受赠人接受时成立，因此胎儿的“接受”须由其法定代理人代为作出。

The gift contract is formed upon acceptance by the donee; therefore, acceptance on behalf of a fetus must be made by its statutory agent.

5. Provided, however, that 但是……除外

Provided, however, that 是法律英语中表示“但书”或“例外条款”的固定表达，相当于中文的“但是”。

"Provided, however, that" is a fixed expression in legal English indicating a proviso or an exception clause, equivalent to "but" in Chinese.

Provided that 表示“以……为条件”，而 Provided, however, that 中的 however 增加了转折意味，强调例外。

"Provided that" means "on condition that," while "Provided, however, that" adds a nuance of contrast through the inclusion of "however," emphasizing the exceptional nature of the clause.

6. ab initio 自始

ab initio 是拉丁语法律术语，意为“自始、从一开始”（从法律拟制的起点起）。

"Ab initio" is a Latin legal term meaning "from the beginning" or "from the very start" (from the starting point of the legal fiction).

在法律英语中，ab initio 常用于表示某行为或状态从最初即无效或不存在。

In legal English, "ab initio" is often used to indicate that an act or a status is void or non-existent from the very outset.
