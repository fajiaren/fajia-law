背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第六条 民事主体从事民事活动，应当遵循公平原则，合理确定各方的权利和义务。

Article 6 When conducting a civil activity, a person of the civil law shall, in compliance with the principle of fairness, reasonably establish the rights and obligations of each party.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第六条，阐明了民事主体各方的权利义务在从事民事活动时的公平性与合理性。

This article, as Article 6 of the Civil Code, sets forth fairness and reasonableness for the rights and obligations of each party when condcuting a civil activity.

公平原则的具体行为标准:在合理范围内确定民事权利与民事义务。

Specific conduct standard for the principle of fairness: to define civil rights and civil obligations within reasonable bounds.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.principle of fairness 公平原则

Fairness侧重交易结果、权利义务配置的实质公允，是成文法基本原则，并非指普通法领域内的equity（衡平），不应直接套入英美衡平法制。

Fairness emphasizes substantive impartiality in transaction outcomes and the allocation of rights and obligations. As a basic principle under the written‑law system, it is not synonymous with equity in the common‑law sphere and shall not be mechanically imported into the Anglo‑American equity regime.

2.establish v. 确定，设定

Establish在民法文本专指：通过民事行为/法律评价，把权利、义务、法律地位予以设定、敲定、予以法律上的确认。

As used in civil‑law texts, “establish” specifically refers to setting, finalising, and granting legal confirmation to rights, obligations and legal status through civil acts or legal evaluation.

当事人从事民事活动，自己去敲定权利义务使用establish；法官裁定权利义务则使用determine。

Where parties themselves settle rights and obligations in the course of conducting a civil activity, the verb establish is used; where a judge fixes such rights and obligations, determine is adopted instead.

3.right n. 权利

一种法律所认可的主体可为、可请求、可受保护的资格/利益。

A legally recognised entitlement of a person to act, demand, or be protected.

power指向支配、公权力；而合同、物权、债权的普通权利只用right。

Power refers to dominion and public‑authority power; for ordinary rights under contracts, real rights and creditor’s rights, only right shall be used.

4.obligation n.义务

指基于合同约定或私法法定而在平等民事主体之间产生的法律拘束。其涵盖全部民事给付义务，不限于金钱欠债。

It denotes a binding legal tie between equal civil subjects arising from contractual agreement or statutory civil‑law provisions. It covers the whole scope of civil duties of performance, not merely monetary indebtedness.

民事约定、法定义务用 obligation；duty则留给公法、道德义务。

Obligation is used for civil contractual stipulations and statutory civil‑law duties; duty is reserved for public‑law and moral obligations.

5.party n.当事人，参与方

指参与特定民事法律关系或交易的自然人、法人。与表征抽象民事权利能力的civil subject不同，party是关系性角色，以参与特定法律事务为前提。

It refers to a natural or juristic person participating in a specific civil‑law relationship or transaction. Unlike a civil subject, which denotes abstract civil‑law capacity, a party is a relational role that presupposes involvement in a given legal affair.

each party适用于双方及多方法律场景，而both parties仅适用于两方主体。

Each party covers two‑party and multi‑party scenarios, whereas both parties applies only where exactly two sides exist.
