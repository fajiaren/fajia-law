背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第八条 民事主体从事民事活动，不得违反法律，不得违背公序良俗。

Article 8 When conducting a civil activity, no person of the civil law shall violate the law, or offend public order or good morals.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第八条，阐明了民事主体各方在从事民事活动时的合法原则与公序良俗原则。

This article, as Article 8 of the Civil Code, sets forth the principle of legality and the principle of public order and good morals for the persons of the civil law when engaging in civil activities.

合法性原则：民事活动不得违反法律（即必须在法律框架内进行）。

The principle of legality:civil activities shall not violate the law (i.e., must be conducted within the legal framework).

公序良俗原则：民事活动不得违背公共秩序和善良风俗（即不得损害社会公共利益和道德风尚）。

The principle of public order and good morals:civil activities shall not offend public order or good morals (i.e., shall not harm public interests or moral standards).

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.no... shall violate 任何……均不得违反

shall在此处与否定词 no搭配，形成 “no... shall violate”的句式，意为“任何……均不得违反”，属于典型的法律禁止性规范。

Here, "shall," used in conjunction with the negative "no," forms the structure "no... shall violate," meaning "no... is permitted to violate," which is a typical prohibitive legal norm.

violate意为“违反、违背”，常与法律（law）、规则（rules）、原则（principles）等搭配使用。强调对法律规范的外在违反行为。

"Violate" means "to contravene or to breach," and is often used with "law," "rules," or "principles," emphasizing an external breach of legal norms.

2.offend v.违背

offend在法律英语中意为“违反、触犯”，与 violate近义，但 offend更常用于违反道德、风俗、公共秩序等非成文法规范。

In legal English, "offend" means "to violate" or "to contravene." It is similar in meaning to "violate," but is more often used in connection with non-statutory norms such as morals, customs, and public order.

3.public order 公共秩序

指国家社会的一般利益，包括国家安全、社会稳定、经济秩序、公共健康等。凡是可能危害社会公共利益的行为，均属违反公序。

It refers to the general interests of the state and society, including national security, social stability, economic order, and public health. Any act that may endanger the public interest constitutes a violation of public order.

在法律实践中，违反公序的行为表现为：妨害国家安全、扰乱社会经济秩序、损害公共健康等。

In legal practice, acts that offend public order include: endangering national security, disrupting social and economic order, and harming public health, among others.

4.good morals 善良风俗

指社会一般道德观念和善良风俗，包括诚实、信义、家庭伦理、性道德等。凡与社会普遍接受的道德观念相悖的行为，均属违反良俗。

It refers to general social moral concepts and good customs, including honesty, fidelity, family ethics, and sexual morality. Any act that runs counter to generally accepted moral standards constitutes a violation of good morals.
