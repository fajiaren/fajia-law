背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第五条 民事主体从事民事活动，应当遵循自愿原则，按照自己的意思设立、变更、终止民事法律关系。

Article 5 When conducting a civil activity, a person of the civil law shall, in compliance with the principle of voluntariness, create, alter, or terminate a civil juristic relationship according to his own will.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第五条，阐明了民事主体在从事民事活动时的意志自主性。

This article, as Article 5 of the Civil Code, sets forth the the autonomy of will of the persons of the civil law when engaging in civil activities.

民事法律关系的三种意思表示：设立、变更、终止。

Three types of declarations of will for civil juristic relationships: establishment, modification, and termination.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.shall v. 应当

“shall”在法律英语中是强制性规范的标志，表示“应当”或“必须”，用于施加法定义务或责任。

n legal English, "shall" is a marker of mandatory norms, meaning "ought to" or "must," and is used to impose legal duties or obligations.

“may”表示“可以”，用于授权性规范中，旨在赋予权利而非施加义务。

In legal English, "may" means "can" or "is permitted to," and is used in permissive norms to confer rights rather than impose obligations.

2.in compliance with 依照、遵守

旨在表示行为主体在行动时主动遵循某一规则、原则或标准，强调“合规性”。此处隐含了“自愿原则”是一项具有规范效力的基本原则。

It means that the actor, in his actions, proactively follows a rule, principle, or standard, emphasizing compliance. Here, it is implied that the principle of voluntariness is a fundamental principle with normative effect.

pursuant to更为正式，常用于引用具体条款授权行为（如“依据第X条”）。

"Pursuant to" is more formal and is often used to refer to specific provisions that authorize conduct, for example, "pursuant to Article X."

3.principle of voluntariness 自愿原则

自愿原则又称“意思自治原则”（principle of autonomy of will），指民事主体在民事活动中自主决定是否设立、变更或终止民事法律关系，任何组织和个人不得非法干预。

The principle of voluntariness is also known as the principle of autonomy of will. It means that the persons of the civil law independently decide whether to create, alter, or terminate a civil juristic relationship in civil activities, and no organization or individual may illegally interfere with such decisions.

4.create v. 设立

“create... a civil juristic relationship”指当事人通过意思表示（如订立合同、作出遗嘱）新设一项法律关系。

"Create... a civil juristic relationship" means that the parties, through a declaration of will (such as concluding a contract or making a will), establish a new legal relationship.

establish更正式，常用于设立机构、制度。

"Establish" is more formal and is often used to set up institutions or systems.

5.alter v. 变更

指对既有民事法律关系的内容或主体进行修改，而不消灭原关系。

It refers to the modification of the content or the parties of an existing civil juristic relationship, without extinguishing the original relationship.

“alter... a civil juristic relationship”指当事人通过新的意思表示，对已设立的法律关系进行修改。

"Alter... a civil juristic relationship" means that the parties, through a new declaration of will, modify an already established legal relationship.

amend常用于修改法律、文件、合同条款。

"Amend" is often used to modify laws, documents, or contractual clauses.

6.terminate v. 终止

指使既有民事法律关系归于消灭，即法律关系不再存在。

It refers to bringing an existing civil juristic relationship to an end, i.e., the relationship no longer exists.

“terminate... a civil juristic relationship”指当事人通过意思表示（如行使解除权）使法律关系消灭。

“Terminate... a civil juristic relationship” means that the parties, through a declaration of will (such as exercising the right of rescission), bring the legal relationship to an end.

extinguish消灭常用于权利消灭。

"Extinguish" is often used to denote the extinction of rights.

7.civil juristic relationship 民事法律关系

指民事主体之间因法律行为（juristic act）所产生的权利义务关系，强调该关系由当事人的意思表示所创设。

It refers to the relationship of rights and obligations between civil subjects arising from a juristic act and emphasizes that the relationship is created by the declaration of will of the parties.

“civil legal relationship”虽然也可译为“民事法律关系”，但范围更广，可包括法律直接规定的关系。

Although “civil legal relationship” may also be translated as “civil juristic relationship,” it has a broader scope and may include relationships directly prescribed by law.

8.according to his own will 按照自己的意思

指当事人基于自身的内心意愿作出法律行为，不受外界强制或不当干预。它是对“自愿原则”最直接的具象化表述。

It means that the parties perform juristic acts based on their own inner will, free from external coercion or improper interference. This is the most direct and concrete expression of the principle of voluntariness.
