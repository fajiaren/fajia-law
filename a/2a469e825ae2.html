背景

BACKGROUND

《中华人民共和国民法典》，由中国的全国人民代表大会制定，于2020年5月28日出台，于2021年1月1日生效施行，全文条款共计一千二百六十条。2021年3月， 全国人民代表大会常务委员会下设的法制工作委员会对《民法典》进行了英语翻译，从而为本刊的《民法典》中英双语解读提供了文本素材与术语标准。

The Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China was enacted by the National People’s Congress of China, adopted on 28 May 2020, and took effect and came into force on 1 January 2021, with a total of 1,260 articles. In March 2021, the Legislative Affairs Commission of the Standing Committee of the National People’s Congress produced an English translation of the Civil Code, thereby providing the textual materials and terminological standards for this journal’s Chinese-English annotated commentary on the Code.

《中华人民共和国民法典》

Civil Code of the People’s Republic of China

第一编　总则

Book One General Part

第一章　基本规定

Chapter I General Provisions

第二条 民法调整平等主体的自然人、法人和非法人组织之间的人身关系和财产关系。

Article 2 The civil law regulates personal and proprietary relationships among the persons of the civil law, namely, natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that are equal in status.

【条款解读】

Provision Interpretation

本条款作为《民法典》第二条，从整个民法范畴出发，说明了民法的规范对象与适用群体。

This article, as Article 2 of the Civil Code, sets forth, from the perspective of the entire civil law, the objects regulated and the persons to whom the civil law applies.

民法的规范对象：平等主体间的人身关系和财产关系。

The objects regulated by the civil law: personal and proprietary relationships among equal subjects.

民法的适用群体：平等主体的自然人、法人和非法人组织。

The persons to whom the civil law applies: natural persons, legal persons, and unincorporated organizations that are equal in status.

【术语解释】

Terminology Explanation

1.civil law 民法

“民法” 是一个法律部门，涵盖所有调整平等主体之间人身关系和财产关系的法律规范总和，不仅包括核心部分的《民法典》，还包括尚未纳入法典或未来可能单行的民事有关法律。

"Civil law" is a legal branch that encompasses the totality of legal norms regulating personal and proprietary relationships among equal subjects. It includes not only the core part, this Civil Code, but also the civil-related laws that have not yet been codified or maybe enacted separately in the future.

2.personal and proprietary relationships 人身关系和财产关系

人身关系personal relationships：指与民事主体的人格、身份不可分离的社会关系。

Personal relationships refer to social relations that are inseparable from the personality and status of the persons of the civil law.

财产关系proprietary relationships:指与财产归属和财产流转相关的社会关系。

Proprietary relationships refer to social relations relating to property ownership and property transfer.

选用“relationships”而非“relations”是因为此处特指具体的关系类型。

The use of "relationships" rather than "relations" is because here it specifically refers to particular types of relations.

3.natural persons 自然人

指基于自然出生而具有民事主体资格的个人。

It refers to individuals who, by virtue of natural birth, possess the status of civil subjects.

不可译为 individuals或 human beings，因后者侧重生物学或社会学，而非法律主体概念。

It should not be translated as "individuals" or "human beings," as those terms emphasize biological or sociological aspects rather than the concept of legal subjects.

4.legal persons 法人

指依法成立，具有民事权利能力和民事行为能力，依法独立享有民事权利和承担民事义务的组织。

It refers to an organization that is established in accordance with law, has capacity for civil rights and capacity for civil conduct, and independently enjoys civil rights and assumes civil obligations in accordance with law.

法人是“法律拟制的人”，依法定程序设立后产生，而非生物学意义上的人。

A legal person is a "a creature of law – a legal fiction", which comes into existence after being established through statutory procedures, rather than being a person in the biological sense.

5.unincorporated organizations 非法人组织

指不具有法人资格，但能够以自己的名义从事民事活动的组织。

It refers to an organization that does not have the status of a legal person, but is able to conduct civil activities in its own name.

依法定程序设立后产生的一类组织（不属于法人），同样并非生物学意义上的主体。

It refers to a category of organizations that are established through statutory procedures and do not qualify as legal persons. Like legal persons, they are not persons in the biological sense either.

6.equal in status 平等主体

指民事主体在法律地位上是平等的，不存在行政上的隶属关系、命令服从关系或特权差异。

It refers to the equal legal status of civil subjects, without any administrative subordination, command-obedience relationships, or differences in privilege.

在法律英语中，status指法律上的身份、资格或地位，而非社会身份或经济状况。

In legal English, "status" refers to legal identity, qualification, or standing, rather than social status or economic condition.
